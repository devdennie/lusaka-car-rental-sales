A stay of 7 days or more uses the weekly rate. 30 days or more uses the monthly rate. Both the in-Lusaka and out-of-town totals are shown. Sample prices.

What you need, and what happens to the deposit

Bring these

NRC or passport. For self-drive, a valid driving licence. A refundable deposit: K2,000 for a hatchback or sedan, K5,000 for an SUV, pickup or van. Sample amounts.

Fuel

Full to full. Collect the car with a full tank and bring it back the same way. Fuel is not included in the sample rate.

Deposit refund

The deposit is returned after check-in if the car is on time, in the same condition, and with the agreed fuel level. It is not part of the rental total.

Driver and airport

Choose self-drive or a driver. A driver is confirmed on WhatsApp. Airport pick-up is at Kenneth Kaunda International.

Pay deposit to reserve

The mobile-money step is a demo. It does not take a payment, a PIN, or a phone number. The only thing that leaves this page is the WhatsApp message you choose to send.

Availability

Booked days on the calendar are sample data, stored on each car in the stock file. They are not a live booking system.
